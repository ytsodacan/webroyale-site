FAQ

Is WebRoyale free?

Yes. WebRoyale is completely free to play. You only need a free account.

Do I need to download anything?

No. The game runs right in your web browser. The game files download once on your first visit and are cached after that.

Which version of Clash Royale is it?

Version 1.9.2 from 2016: the classic cards, arenas and look of early Clash Royale.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. WebRoyale works in modern browsers on desktop and mobile. Chrome, Edge and Firefox work best.

Who will I battle against?

Real players in real time. If nobody else is queued after a minute, you get a match against a training bot so you are never stuck waiting.
